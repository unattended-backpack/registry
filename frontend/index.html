Ethereum Foundation Registry

An onchain directory of Ethereum Foundation personnel. Profiles are private and require a proven @ethereum.org email to manage. All proofs are locally-generated in the browser.

This should be your own node, ideally a local one. Every read and every check of a transaction goes through it; the page contacts nothing else.

Register a profile

  1. 1. Your profile secret

    The secret makes your profile yours and nobody else's to find. It never leaves this browser. Save it somewhere safe: every renewal needs it. Lose it and your profile lapses at its next renewal; register a fresh one then.

    or paste one you already have

    3. Send the email
    
        
    From your @ethereum.org address, send an email
        whose subject is exactly that line. The body can be
        anything. Send it to another inbox you can read:
        mail you send to your own address is never DKIM signed. Open it there
        and download the original message ("Download original" in Gmail) as
        an .eml file. The subject reveals nothing: only your
        secret opens it.

    5. Prepare the registration, then broadcast it, ideally later
    
        
    Registration needs no signature: the proof is the
        authority, so anyone may broadcast it. The chain sees when it lands,
        and anyone who can read your mailbox sees when you sent the email. To
        keep those two apart, copy the transaction and broadcast it hours or
        days later, from an account with no link to you or through a relayer.
        It stays valid as long as the email does: four weeks.

Records

    
Edit any value in place, add new records, or empty a value
    to clear its record. Every change goes out together: the controller signs
    once, and anyone may broadcast the result, so the controller never needs
    funds.

Renew, or rotate the controller

    
Renew at least every 90 days: send a fresh email
    authorizing your current controller again. To rotate, authorize a new
    controller instead. Either way the current controller co-signs, and
    your secret (above) builds the subject line.
